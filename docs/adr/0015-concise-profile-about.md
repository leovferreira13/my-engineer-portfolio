## Context

The expanded About repeated employer-specific evidence already available in experience and capabilities. The owner prefers a short LinkedIn-style introduction without subsections or a focus on any company. This supersedes ADR 0014 while retaining its approach to detailed experience and capabilities.

## Decision

**Use a concise, continuous About centered on the professional profile in all five languages.** Keep Product Engineer as the primary positioning, with adaptability, customer understanding, ownership, backend foundations, and applied AI woven into three paragraphs. Employer names, detailed metrics, tools, and project evidence belong in the existing experience, capability, and project sections.

Retain curated multilingual capability explanations and English machine exports from ADR 0014. Preserve the scope of all experience metrics and the appointment agent's prelaunch status. The English machine About reuses the same profile copy.

## Options considered

- **Short profile narrative (chosen):** gives a quick introduction and leaves evidence to the detailed sections.
- Employer-led subsections: useful detail but repetitive and too long for the introduction.
- A list of target job titles: makes the positioning harder to understand.

## Consequences

The About is easier to scan without section headings. Updates still require equivalent translations and refreshed dark-mode captures. Revisit its length when the primary professional positioning changes, rather than adding each new experience to the introduction.
