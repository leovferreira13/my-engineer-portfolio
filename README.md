## About me

I am a **Product Engineer** with **5+ years** across backend and data engineering, product management, and technical leadership. I connect user and business needs to practical software, taking problems from discovery through technical decisions, implementation, launch, and iteration.

**Adaptability is the thread through my work.** I enjoy learning an unfamiliar domain, listening to the people who know it, and working through ambiguity to understand what is useful to build. I am comfortable moving between customer conversations, product trade-offs, and the technical details that make a solution work in practice.

I bring strong **backend foundations** and product judgment to that process, with experience in APIs, data pipelines, integrations, and event-driven systems. More recently, I have been applying that background to **AI agents**, focusing on how they connect to real workflows, behave reliably, and can be evaluated and improved. I work best close to the problem, with ownership of both the engineering and the experience it creates.

## What I bring

- **Product and customer workflows:** discovery, user research, technical scoping, prioritization, and delivery across scheduling, medical records, prescriptions, and pharmacy workflows.
- **Backend and data systems:** Python, FastAPI, Django, PostgreSQL, Redis, AWS, and event-driven processing; a data engineering foundation with Azure, Databricks, and PySpark.
- **Applied AI:** multi-turn agents with LangGraph, OpenAI tool calling, RAG, scheduling integrations, behavioral evaluations, guardrails, and observability. The appointment agent is in prelaunch testing; customer outcomes are still to be measured after launch.

## Selected work

### Football, Visualized

An interactive football analytics app that makes expected goals tangible: change the conditions of a shot, inspect the model's features, and explore the estimate. I connected a reproducible StatsBomb Open Data pipeline to XGBoost training and evaluation, browser inference, and a five-language Next.js playground.

**[Try the app](https://sports-data.leoferreira.dev.br/en)** · **[Explore the source](https://github.com/leovferreira13/sports-data-visualizer)**

This is my first public project. [The project catalog](https://leoferreira.dev.br/en/projects) will grow as I publish more work.
