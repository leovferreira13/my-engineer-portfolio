## Context

The generic introduction delayed recognition of the primary engineering specialty and left leadership versus individual contribution ambiguous. Supersedes ADR 0015's About narrative while preserving its concise format and separation from employer-specific evidence.

## Decision

**Keep Product Engineer as the headline identity and identify Python backend as the specialty immediately below the name.** The owner-selected headline is "Product Engineer | SaaS, Applied AI & Backend Systems". Remove the generic discovery-to-production tagline. About explains backend skills, current individual contributor direction with product and leadership background, and applied AI work in three paragraphs, equivalently across all five languages. Detailed employer outcomes remain in experience.

## Options considered

- **Specialty followed by product context (chosen):** makes the profile easier to classify while preserving its distinguishing experience.
- Multiple equal role titles: leave the primary direction ambiguous.
- Employer-led About: duplicates experience and adds length.

## Consequences

The English export and README share the same About narrative. Future copy must preserve the distinction between engineering, product management, and leadership experience. Revisit if the owner changes career direction.
